How it works
How it works

Every coin ends.Here's how.

01

The dev sets a lifespan.

Anywhere from 24 hours to 7 days, set once at launch.

02

It trades against the clock.

The countdown is on the coin the whole time. Everyone can see it coming.

03

At zero, it pays out.

Trading closes and the whole pool goes back to holders, split by how much each one holds.

No slow bleed to zero.
Just a date.
